# Money Honey v2 – Product Requirements

## Vision

Money Honey is an iPhone-first personal finance app that combines a traditional checkbook register with modern budgeting, bill tracking, and debt payoff tools.

The app should feel warm, clean, cozy, and easy to use.

## Core Goals

- Track accounts and balances
- Add income, expenses, transfers, and debt payments
- Show a running balance after every transaction
- Track bills and income on a calendar
- Automatically reduce credit card debt when payments are entered
- Allow custom categories, payees, and icons
- Support beautiful saved themes
- Save data locally with import/export backup

## Version 1.0 Features

### Home
- Net worth
- Available cash
- Upcoming bills
- Recent transactions
- Debt snapshot

### Accounts
- Account list
- Individual checkbook registers
- Running balances
- Add, edit, and delete transactions
- Transfers between accounts

### Bills
- Monthly bill calendar
- Recurring bills
- Income items
- Mark bills as paid
- Move bill dates

### Debt
- Credit card and loan tracking
- Current balance
- Minimum payment
- Due date
- Automatic balance reduction from payments

### Budget
- Monthly category budgets
- Progress bars
- Remaining amount

### Settings
- Accounts
- Categories
- Payees
- Themes
- Backup
- Import/export

## Must-Have Behaviors

- Themes must save and remain active across the whole app.
- Category icons must show on related bills and transactions.
- The plus button should add items relevant to the current page.
- Editing a transaction date should move it correctly.
- Credit card payments should update debt balances automatically.
- Income should appear in the Bills section as a positive item.

## Design Direction

Money Honey should feel:

- Warm
- Soft
- Modern
- Cozy
- Organized
- iPhone-friendly
- Not corporate
